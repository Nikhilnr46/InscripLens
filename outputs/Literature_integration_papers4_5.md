# Literature Integration: Papers 4 & 5

This document details the practical integration of two foundational epigraphic research papers into the InscripLens project pipeline.

---

## 1. Paper Attributions & Core Findings

### Paper 4: AI-Powered Ancient Inscription Analysis and Translation (Halmidi)
- **Citation:** Prekshitha K R, Karthik Kumar S V, Kushaal B, Ponnamma K M, Dr. Ayesha Taranum. *ITM Web of Conferences 81, 01020 (2026)*, ETMIS 2025. DOI: 10.1051/itmconf/20268101020.
- **Core Contribution:** An end-to-end Halegannada (Old Kannada) analysis and translation framework tested on the 5th-century CE Halmidi inscription.
- **Key Mechanism:** Combines CNN feature extraction with recurrent sequence modeling (CRNN) to tolerate ligatures, coupled with a two-stage NLP translation pipeline:
  1. *Stage 1:* Halegannada to Modern Kannada using rule-based morphological normalization and historical dictionaries.
  2. *Stage 2:* Modern Kannada to English using transformer-based Neural Machine Translation (NMT).

### Paper 5: Unveiling Text in Challenging Stone Inscriptions: A Character-Context-Aware Patching Strategy for Binarization
- **Citation:** Pratyush Jena, Amal Joseph, Arnav Sharma, Ravi Kiran Sarvadevabhatla (CVIT, IIIT Hyderabad). *ACM Indian Conference on Computer Vision, Graphics, and Image Processing (ICVGIP 2025)*, Mandi, India. ACM ISBN: 979-8-4007-1930-1/25/12.
- **Core Contribution:** Foundational paper for the **Shilalekhya Attention U-Net binarizer** utilized in InscripLens (`work/shilalekhya/binarizer.pth`).
- **Key Mechanism:** Character-Context-Aware Patching (inter-quartile connected component height calculation $h_{cc}$, adaptive kernel dilation) and a two-stage self-refining inference pipeline (multi-scale sliding windows across scales 256, 384, 512, 768 with max-fusion).

## 2. Implementations Integrated into InscripLens

### A. GPU-Accelerated Multi-Scale Binarizer (`outputs/binarizer_core.py`)
Following Paper 5's *Algorithm 1 (Self-Refining Inference Pipeline)*:
1. **CUDA Auto-Detection:** Automatically leverages `cuda:0` (NVIDIA RTX 3050 Laptop GPU) when available, falling back gracefully to 4-thread CPU.
2. **Multi-Scale Sliding Window (`enhance_multiscale`):**
   - Evaluates input stone images across multiple patch scales ($S = \{256, 384, 512\}$) with an 80% stride.
   - Resizes extracted patches to $512 \times 512$ for the Attention U-Net.
   - Projects predicted sigmoid probabilities back to original spatial scales.
   - Performs max-probability fusion across pyramid scales to reconstruct complete character strokes while suppressing background stone noise.
3. **Execution Benchmarks (on Nagavara Demonstration Stone HS027, $525 \times 1080$):**
   - *Legacy CPU Single-Scale:* ~19.0 seconds.
   - *GPU Single-Scale:* 1.24 seconds (~15x speedup).
   - *GPU Multi-Scale (Paper 5):* 4.98 seconds, reducing noisy fragmented components from 133 down to 118 high-confidence character regions.

### B. Two-Stage Epigraphic Translation Engine (`outputs/translation_core.py`)
Following Paper 4's *Two-Stage Translation Architecture*:
1. **Stage 1: Halegannada to Modern Kannada Normalization:**
   - *Phonological Shifts:* Classical indigenous /p/ to modern /h/ (*pālu* -> *hālu*, *poḻal* -> *hoḷalu*, *pū* -> *hū*, *pesaru* -> *hesaru*, *pari* -> *hari*).
   - *Case Suffix Normalization:* Archaic accusative (*-am*, *-an* -> *-annu*), instrumental/ablative (*-im*, *-indam* -> *-inda*), locative (*-oḷ*, *-ul* -> *-alli*), genitive (*-ā* -> *-ina*).
   - *Verb Paradigms:* Past honorific third-person plural (*-ār*, *-dār*, *-idār* -> *-daru*), optative (*-ge*, *-kku* -> *-ali*, *-agali*).
   - *Consonant Mergers:* Archaic retroflex consonants (*ḻ* -> *ḷ*, *ṟ* -> *r*).
2. **Stage 2: Contextual English Interpretation:**
   - Detects formulaic epigraphic structures:
     - Regnal formulas: *svasti śrī pṛthivī-rājyam geye* ("Hail! While the king was ruling the sovereignty of the earth:").
     - Battle & Cattle-raid context: *kāḷagadoḷ turugōḷ* ("In the battle during an armed cattle raid,").
     - Heroic martyrdom: *sarggamēṟidār* ("the hero fought valiantly and ascended to heaven").
     - Commemoration: *kalla niṟisidār* ("erected this memorial stone in his honor").
     - Land grants: *bāḷgaccu koṭṭodu* ("bestowed a sword-washing tax-exempt land grant").
3. **API Endpoint (`api/server.py`):**
   - Added `POST /api/translate` allowing REST clients to submit transliterated or recognized inscriptions and receive structured token analysis, modernized Kannada transcriptions, genre classification, and contextual English translations.

## 3. Scope Boundaries & Research Ethics
In line with `outputs/STUDY_GUIDE.md`:
- **Binarization masks** predict foreground pixel locations; they do not automatically isolate individual character semantics.
- **Modern Kannada & English outputs** generated by `translation_core.py` represent rule- and context-guided computational hypotheses, not certified ground truth.
- True epigraphical decipherment on degraded hero stones requires human-in-the-loop expert review using the InscripLens Studio inspector.
